# The Short-Form Content Bible

What 8 short-form creators teach about making videos people actually watch, in one book: 20 chapters built from
463 of their Instagram posts (April to July 2026). Every claim links to the original reel, so you can check it
yourself. Where the creators disagree, both sides are shown side by side instead of averaged.

## Read it

- **Styled version**, with clickable sources: [`index.html`](index.html). Download it and open it in a browser.
- **Markdown version**, readable right here on GitHub: [`short-form-content-bible.md`](short-form-content-bible.md).

## Use it with Claude or ChatGPT

Paste this, with your script or video idea under it:

```text
Read https://raw.githubusercontent.com/christianjohnston-ai/short-form-content-bible/main/short-form-content-bible.md and judge my short-form script below against it. Cite the chapter and section for every note.
```

## What is inside

- **Part 0, Strategy:** pick your game before you pick tactics.
- **Part I, Foundations:** the inner game, personal brand, niche and positioning.
- **Part II, The Craft:** topics, the copying spectrum, hooks, scripting, retention (including 8.8, Rehooks: the
  40% valley), delivery, filming and editing.
- **Part III, The Machine:** the algorithm, volume and trial reels, analytics.
- **Part IV, The Business:** growth formats, CTAs and monetization, teams and tools, AI, applied playbooks.
- **Part V, Long-Form Companion:** packaging and YouTube.

## Where it comes from

- The 8 creators: Zach (personalbrandtips), Sean Stewart, Adley, Jessi Jean, Bonusfootage, Briar Cochran,
  Max Tornow and Layton Video. Their teachings are theirs; each one links back to the post it came from.
- Two outside sources are tagged in the text and never mixed with those citations: an interview with Kane Callaway
  ("Kallaway ▶") and a short video on rehooks ("Rehooks ▶").
- Connecting text marked *[synthesis]* is the editor's, not a creator's.
